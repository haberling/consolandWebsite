# ScanMan

A barcode scanner clipboard utility. Point a USB/serial scanner at it, and every scan gets copied straight to your clipboard — no window to babysit, it just sits in the system tray.

`v1.0.0` out for windows.

## Features

1. Lives in the system tray — no window to keep open
2. Remembers your COM port between runs
3. Toast notification on every scan
4. Restart/reconfigure from the tray menu, no reinstall needed
