# Registering consoland.net on Porkbun

Walkthrough for buying the domain. Pricing checked 2026-08-14: ~$12.52/yr, no bait-and-switch renewal pricing.

## Steps

1. Go to **porkbun.com** and search `consoland.net` in the homepage search bar.
2. Confirm it shows as available with a price, then **Add to Cart**.
3. Create an account (or log in) — just email + password.
4. In the cart, review the free add-ons:
   - **WHOIS privacy** — free, leave it on (keeps your name/address off public WHOIS)
   - **SSL certificate** — free, fine to include if useful later
   - Skip anything paid you don't need
5. Set registration length — 1 year is fine; longer terms just prepay future years at the same rate.
6. Checkout with card or PayPal. One-time charge for the term chosen, no subscription.
7. Once purchased, the domain appears in the Porkbun dashboard. From there you can point nameservers or add DNS records.
