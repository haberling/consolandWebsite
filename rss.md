# Consoland Feed

A curated what's-new list. Each `##` heading is one item in `/rss.xml`
and in the homepage What's New section.
Required fields are `date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) and `url` (a site route, no
leading slash, same form as nav paths). `author` is optional. The first
paragraph after the fields is the summary.

Newest date wins; same-day items keep the order they appear here.
